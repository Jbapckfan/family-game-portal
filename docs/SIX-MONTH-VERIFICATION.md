## Scope and remaining limits

“Distinct” means different whole-board configurations, not182 independently authored ideas. Some word games reuse vocabulary, clues, categories, arches or letter racks in new combinations. Tangle retains uncommon dictionary words; the lexical repair is a bounded reviewed list, not a complete human dictionary review. Human difficulty/wording and physical iPhone touch/audio/haptics remain unverified.

The protected calendar ends April28. A further guaranteed fresh window requires another content/calendar release; this is not a perpetual rolling guarantee. Kimi practice rounds are in-memory and reset on reload; all six daily saves persist. The other26 games preserve practice saves. Browser localStorage guards do not provide atomic cross-process/multi-key transactions. Saves are tied to browser and origin, and the local preview uses its own profile.
