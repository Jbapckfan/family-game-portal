## A · PRECISION

Label outside the app at the very top "A · PRECISION", in small modern sans-serif. Design direction: the restraint and material intelligence of a modern Apple-designed titanium instrument. Matte white architecture, plenty of intentional breathing room, almost invisible rules, compact very-low-radius segmented controls. Tokens are dark bead-blasted titanium with thin colored ceramic inlays and precision-cut satin aluminum chamfers. Green, wine-red and champagne inlays indicate the puzzle colors without large bright faces. No ornate borders, no heavy shiny picture frames, no oversized rounded plastic trays. Five examples sit on a single clean white field with fine aligned indices. Prediction positions are understated thin-outlined squares. Answer choices are quietly raised white plates with dark pieces, separated through spacing. Elegant minimal SANS-SERIF title, small clean metadata. Crisp contrast and extraordinary precision.

## B · PERFORMANCE

## C · TITANIUM

Label outside the app at the very top "C · TITANIUM", in small modern sans-serif. Design direction: blend Apple's quiet material sophistication with Audi's crisp precision. Strong contemporary SANS-SERIF typography, clean white surface and pale blue collection backdrop. Five independent physical graphite-titanium objects with a delicate colored inner facet and a hairline bright machined edge. They appear lifted a few millimeters off the white surface, with precise soft shadows. Favor clean chamfered geometry, a restrained silver-gray finish and rich controlled color, not jewelry, candy or rounded blobs. The sequence and three prediction positions share one open, elegant white composition, with very few visible containers. Use fine gray horizontal baselines and small indices to establish the order. Compact, modern, exact, editorial without serif fonts, physically convincing without ornamental skeuomorphism.
