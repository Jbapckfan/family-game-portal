# Built-in image-generation prompts

Each image used the shared prompt below followed by its option-specific paragraph. No API/CLI fallback was used. These are visual concepts for selection.

## Shared prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a polished visual concept, not code, for an adult daily pattern-recognition puzzle called "Pattern Prism", within "Alford Daily". Render a crisp, front-facing desktop/web game interface, portrait composition with the complete game board visible and legible. This is a UI design proposal, not a photo of a phone or a tablet; no device frame. Product palette and background are fixed: very pale cool blue #eff5fb with a subtle icy blue radial glow at upper right, white panels, dark navy readable text #123a54, restrained teal accents. The page must feel cohesive with a premium light daily-puzzle collection. Do not use a dark page, dark play surface, purple, brown, wood, cartoon styling, toy plastic, confetti, decorative stars, or a new product name. Strong adult art direction, exact spacing, excellent typography, sophisticated physical materials, meaningful soft studio lighting. All objects have subtle real thickness, grounded contact shadows and precise edges. Text must be readable rather than abundant. Header text: "Alford Daily", then title "Pattern Prism", small Today / Practice switch. Main section "Read the sequence". Five clearly numbered examples 01–05, then a "Continue the sequence" section with three empty positions 06–08; 06 is active with a question mark. Then "What belongs at 6?" and four options A B C D. Five example objects in this exact order: 01 small sage-green circle arrow east, 02 small champagne-gold five-point star arrow east, 03 small muted-rose triangle arrow north, 04 small sage-green square arrow north, 05 small champagne-gold circle arrow east. Four answer options in order: A small muted-rose star east, B small muted-rose circle east, C small champagne-gold star east, D larger muted-rose star south. Keep direction arrows separate and visible; don't rotate light/shadows with object direction. Color, shape, size and direction are actual puzzle clues. Include concise labels under pieces, and a small "A little hint" button. No fake score, no IQ number, no invented claims. No provider or model names, no watermark. Do not show a selected correct answer. Only vary the specified materials/layout; keep the above background and exact puzzle consistent.

## A — Porcelain

Design option A, labeled outside the app at top "A · PORCELAIN". A luxurious porcelain and enamel puzzle: softly glazed ceramic pieces with colored enamel faces, refined rounded chamfers, a crisp white outer edge and subtle porcelain side wall. Not glossy candy. Shallow molded white ceramic wells hold the pieces. The sequence is a refined 5-position tray; option choices are four separate generous white cards. More spacious editorial composition, airy negative space, gentle shadows, large easy-to-read labels. Fine tasteful borders, no industrial clutter. Physical yet calm and adult.

## B — Cut glass

Design option B, labeled outside the app at top "B · CUT GLASS". Distinct sculptural optical-glass puzzle pieces: thick translucent cut crystal with subtle bevel refraction, frosted interiors in muted sage/rose/champagne, sharp sophisticated edges, realistic transmission, very restrained highlights. No neon. The pieces sit freely on one continuous satin-white display tray with faint position marks instead of a box around every example. Three prediction recesses, and glass answer objects on a unified lower selection ledge. A beautifully restrained contemporary gallery feeling. Flat frontal UI, physical pieces seen slightly from above so their thickness is clear. Labels remain readable navy outside glass.

## C — Machined metal

Design option C, labeled outside the app at top "C · MACHINED METAL". Precise satin anodized metal tokens with subtle brushed finish, champagne/sage/rose coloration, clean chamfered bright aluminum rims and visible solid side walls. Expert industrial design, mature and tactile, not coins with stamped icons: each object itself is the circle, square, triangle or five-point star. A single finely recessed white instrument tray for the five examples, clean slim separators and carefully aligned indices. A concise lower prediction/selection area with raised white answer plates, dark readable labels, fine blue-gray rules. More compact and structured than porcelain, less reflective than glass. Elegant, deliberate, minimal, looks manufacturable and excellent for touch. No exposed screws or unnecessary dashboard gauges.
