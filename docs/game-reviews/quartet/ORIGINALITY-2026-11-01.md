## Finding

The current Quartet is mechanically very close to NYT Connections. It should not be described as a wholly original game or cleared for launch on the strength of its different presentation.

| Element | Current Quartet | Published Connections rules |
|---|---|---|
| Central challenge | Sixteen words; identify four groups of four | Sixteen words; identify common-theme groups of four |
| Attempts | Four incorrect submissions end the round | Completion requires fewer than four mistakes |
| Reveals | Correct groups lock and reveal a theme; four numbered tiers | Groups have four color-coded difficulty levels |
| Rhythm and result | Daily board, solved groups, shareable guess rows | Daily puzzle, completion statistics and shareable results |

Comparison source, accessed November 1, 2026: [The New York Times Help Center — Connections](https://thenewyorktimeshelpcenter.helpjuice.com/360011158491-New-York-Times-Games/28525912587924-Connections). Its About, When to Play, Statistics and Share Your Results sections support the comparison above. The help.nytimes.com article and live game could not be opened by the research tool; this hosted Help Center copy was readable. No competitor puzzles were imported.

Quartet additionally has a persistent pair-glow hint, separate practice rounds and blue/teal numbered styling. These are real differences, but do not materially change the central grouping decision. The local instructions also use “One away…” feedback; that was inspected in Quartet, but was not documented in the readable comparison source and is not needed for this finding.
