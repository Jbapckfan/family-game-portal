## How to play

Use every one of the ten tiles exactly once to make two words matching their clues. Choose a word row, then tap rack letters or type them. When a row fills, entry moves to the other row. Tap a placed letter to return it to the rack; duplicate letters remain separate tiles. Check words when both rows are full.

On a keyboard, Up/Down chooses the row, letters enter available tiles, and Backspace returns a letter. Enter on the focused puzzle checks the arrangement. Hints keep correct openings, clear incorrect endings and add one letter. Reveal is distinct from a solve.

Split accepts its prepared clue-specific answer pair. It is not an unrestricted dictionary game; an alternative answer requires evaluating both clues and the shared rack.
