# Sprout — answers integrated into the plant

Built-in image-generation tool; original concept mockups. Shared prompt followed by the corresponding variant.

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a radically improved original Sprout adult word-game UI for Alford Daily. USER'S ESSENTIAL REQUIREMENT: THE ANSWERS ARE PART OF THE PLANT ITSELF. Words must physically form leaves, branches or the vine; they are not a separate history list and not rectangular word cards pasted over a plant photo. Show a beautiful coherent, living, sculptural botanical game object built from accepted answers, where each answer visibly grows the plant. Sophisticated tactile 3D botanical illustration, refined light and detailed leaf veins, excellent clear contemporary sans-serif typography. No serif type, cartoon faces, emoji, toy blocks, wood/brown UI, purple, rainbow, external brands or newspaper references.
Cohesion: keep the collection's very pale cool blue #eff5fb background, white/pale surfaces, navy #123a54 text, dark evergreen accents, a restrained white ceramic seed bed at the bottom of the plant. Original portrait-format complete app UI, straight on, no device frame. Plenty of intentional space, not a cluttered diagram. Plant must be the actual puzzle board, with clear reading order and words large enough to read. No floating rectangle answer boxes or plaques across foliage. No separate accepted-word timeline anywhere.
Exact illustrative state, not the user's daily puzzle: accepted seed CAT, then CATS, then CASTS. Exactly those three completed words must be clearly readable ON/AS the plant, ascending from the seed to the newest growth. It is currently a 5-letter plant, with 3 steps left before the 8-letter bloom. A fresh growing tip or young curled leaf is the input location for the next 6-letter word; mark it subtly "6 letters". No open flower yet; a single closed ivory bud is okay. Do not display answers for 6,7,8 letter stages. Basic control area below the plant: current letters C A S T S, remaining soil letters E R T, one Grow button, quiet Clear / Undo / Restart and Hint · 1 wilt. A concise label says "Tap letters or type". Do not add a separate large answer field or word-history list. Status text only: "3 steps to bloom" and "5 wilts left"; no dot counters or fake scores. Header small Alford Daily, title Sprout, compact Today / Practice tabs.


## A · WORD LEAVES

Design A, label outside app at top "A · WORD LEAVES". Each complete answer is visibly inscribed into the surface of one broad, beautiful leaf, as though the letters are part of its pale vein structure. Lowest mature leaf bears CAT, middle leaf bears CATS, newest upper leaf bears CASTS. These are large organic pointed leaves with curved edges and natural folds, each joined to one continuous graceful central stem; alternate directions, but the words remain horizontal and easy to read. Rich deep green leaf surfaces, readable ivory lettering, carefully controlled veins around rather than through glyphs. The answer-bearing leaves progressively grow longer with the word. A curled young leaf above them is the six-letter input/growth location. Nothing looks like a card or plaque. The plant has real asymmetry and convincing volume, not three repeated flat SVG ribbons. Elegant, immediately understandable word-as-leaf concept.

## B · LETTER BRANCHES

Design B, label outside app at top "B · LETTER BRANCHES". Every LETTER is carried by a real leaf attached to an organic branch, and each completed answer becomes one gently curved word branch. The low branch spells C A T across three broad leaf surfaces, the middle branch spells C A T S across four, and the highest completed branch spells C A S T S across five. Make the leaves naturally attached with petioles, rich green 3D veins and soft shadows; they are not rectangular tiles with a leaf icon. Give each word branch enough white space to be read left-to-right, with a tiny 3/4/5 numeral at its stem junction if useful. A single fresh curled shoot above marks the next six-letter branch without showing any answer. The branches grow from one central plant into a balanced open canopy. Natural botanical shapes, clear lettering, slightly playful tactile interaction, still an adult premium puzzle app.

## C · LIVING WORD VINE

Design C, label outside app at top "C · LIVING WORD VINE". Push the integration further: the readable uppercase word forms THEMSELVES are sculpted from smooth living green stems and leaves, connected into one climbing vine. Three gently horizontal sections ascending from the planter clearly spell CAT, CATS and CASTS. The shapes of the letters act like organic stem junctions, with a few elegant real leaves sprouting from their strokes. The letters are not printed on cards and not merely overlaid labels: they are the plant's physical growth. Make this a sophisticated botanical typographic sculpture, smooth green surfaces with subtle veins and pale rim light, not a novelty comic font or twisted illegible topiary. Keep the word silhouettes extremely legible. A curved fresh stem-tip at top is marked subtly "6 letters" for the next growth. One continuous natural plant body, creative, distinctive, satisfying, grounded in a white ceramic seed bed.
