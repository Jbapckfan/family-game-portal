Render the entire UI straight on in portrait format, no device frame, no angled screenshot, no newspaper or publisher references, no third-party logos.

Precise alignment and spacing, clear visual hierarchy, grown-up sophistication. No dark theme, purple, brown UI, wooden frames, rainbow, toy plastic, smiley faces, emoji or fake currencies. Physical detail belongs to the plant, with natural leaf shapes, nuanced light and believable organic growth. Not a repeated ladder of identical symmetrical leaves. No words, letter tiles, labels or cards attached to or covering leaves, flowers or stems. Keep text readable and high contrast.

The plant grows on accepted words and opens a flower only at the eight-letter finish. The design shown is MID-GROWTH at five letters; show healthy leaves and a closed bud, not the final open flower. Display "3 steps to bloom" and five small remaining-wilt markers; a letter hint costs one wilt. Include quiet Clear, Undo and Restart controls, and a small "Hint · 1 wilt" control if space. Do not add gameplay rules, scores, timers, daily streak charts or an accepted guess not in the specified state.
Header: small "Alford Daily", strong simple title "Sprout", compact Today / Practice switch. Original rich plant artwork and carefully designed usable word entry are both important. No giant unused word slots for levels 7 and 8 and no tall blank ladder. Show sufficient plant detail at mid-stage to be interesting immediately. Keep the layout composed and legible, with no clipped bottom controls.
