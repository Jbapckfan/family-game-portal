## Playing

Follow the clue to form a nine-letter word on the 3×3 board. Consecutive tiles must touch horizontally, vertically or diagonally, and a tile can be used only once. Tap an earlier selected tile to shorten the path, or use Undo/Clear. Submit with Check trail. Keyboard arrow keys move tile focus; Space or Enter selects; Backspace undoes and Escape clears. There is no timer.

Trail accepts clue-specific prepared answers, not every word in a general dictionary. The underlying shared runtime retains separate Daily/Practice results and spoiler-free sharing. An additional accepted spelling or valid alternative clue solution would require editorial verification rather than automatic corpus admission.
