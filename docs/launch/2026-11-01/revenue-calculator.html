Formulas and interpretation

Ads = daily active players × days × free share × eligible opportunities × displayed share × realized publisher eCPM ÷ 1,000

New purchases = new eligible players × conversion
Purchase receipts = new purchases × one-time price × (1 − fee)
After listed costs = ads + purchase receipts − operating cost − labor cost

A fractional buyer is an expectation in this arithmetic model, not an actual transaction. The break-even purchase count is rounded up. This model does not infer that better ads or a higher price will leave player retention and conversion unchanged.
