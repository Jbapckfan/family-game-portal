# Alford Daily social and screenshot drafts

Lead with a recognizable action in one game and the player's result. Use actual gameplay and actual results; obtain permission before using another person's score or quote. Published links must use the final verified game URL. Keep answers, puzzle solutions and future Daily boards out of marketing captures.

## Launch posts

**Collection introduction**

Your next favorite daily puzzle might be a word chain, a number path, or a connection you almost missed. Alford Daily brings word and logic games together in one place. Pick a game, find your rhythm, and share your result without spoiling the answer. Play: [verified launch URL]

**Challenge invitation**

One puzzle. Same day. Different approaches. Try today's [game name], then share your result and tell us which move made it click. [verified game URL]

**Behind the game**

A good word game should accept the words that fit its rules. We're expanding our checked vocabulary, improving clues and making each game's next move clearer. Try the beta and tell us where a puzzle surprises you—or gets in your way. [verified beta URL]

**A player's choice**

Which would you play again: a satisfying letter trail, a high-low number path, or a word ladder? Try Alford Daily and choose your favorite. [verified launch URL]

**Result caption pattern**

Alford Daily · [game] · [played Central date]
[actual outcome and aggregate result]
[assistance disclosure when used]
Your turn: [verified exact game URL]

Do not replace the actual caption with invented perfect scores. Sharing stays optional and never grants a puzzle advantage.

## Store screenshots

Use screenshots from the actual signed iOS build on the required device sizes; web captures cannot stand in for native-app evidence. Apple's product page supports up to ten screenshots and a subtitle of up to thirty characters. [Apple product-page guide](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/), [required dimensions](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications)

Capture a clear first action at normal text size and an accessibility-size example for the press kit. Do not cover clues, controls, creator credits or required disclosures with headlines. Exclude an App Store badge until there is a genuine store destination.

## Publication sequence

1. Private beta: show one mechanic, observe first play, ask which game people voluntarily return to.
2. Polish update: demonstrate a concrete improvement from feedback and invite another round.
3. Launch announcement: publish the verified website link and actual app availability, price and ad behavior.
4. First week: rotate demonstrations among reviewed keepers; share real player results with permission.
5. Second week: compare measured completion, return visits and sharing by game. Promote the favorites that earn repeat play.

Prepare the posts and clips now. Publishing, contacting creators/testers and purchasing promotion require their own authorized action.
