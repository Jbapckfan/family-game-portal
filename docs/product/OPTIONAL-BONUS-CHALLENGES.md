# Optional harder bonus challenges

James requested an optional harder level for games where it makes sense. This is a product plan, not a claim that every game already has a tested bonus bank.

## Consistent behavior

Offer **Try the bonus challenge** after a regular round, and from Practice. Entry is always optional. Bonus records, assistance and shares must be labeled and stored separately from the daily result; a bonus never changes a daily streak. Preserve every published board and old save. Use familiar vocabulary and meaningful decisions; a tighter timer or more obscure words alone is not sufficient. Touch/keyboard pacing must be considered separately in timed games. Verify unique solutions where promised, accept legitimate alternatives, and validate each dated bonus bank before release.

## Current entry points

- Ringcount / Full Circle now has a separately saved **Tougher bonus**, reachable from the game or `?sample=tougher`. Same twelve numbers and four cuts; more misleading target-sum arcs and uneven section lengths. One reviewed sample, not a new daily calendar.
- Kimi Tangle has a 5×5 sample. Its larger-board calendar remains future work.
- James prefers the harder Trail and Split samples. Those preferences do not automatically turn their existing sample banks into production calendars, or imply a further bonus has been built.
- Tic-Tac-Toe already offers Casual and Tactical AI. A further bonus should have a distinctive tactical challenge; it should not duplicate an existing difficulty button.
