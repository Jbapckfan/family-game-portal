## Limits for the individual reviews

Full source membership includes rare, regional, technical, archaic and some proper-name entries; it does not establish familiar usage or eliminate every questionable word. Tangle's source-wide alternative partitions include such entries. The generator retains an explicit finite policy; it does not infer a complete names/slurs policy from spelling.

Expanding Sprout's real continuations leaves **136 of 211** ecosystems with no reachable valid-word dead-end branch, with a range of **0–217** such edges. They still require finding five legal growth words, but old trap counts no longer describe v2 difficulty. The implementation does not reject legitimate words to manufacture traps. Human difficulty calibration and future content selection remain required.

Quartet's 420 boards have unique tiles, valid tiers and one complete partition under every authored category's known member pool. There are no known-pool cross-category alternatives. This does not prove uniqueness under every possible human meaning or certify its author-assigned difficulty tiers. Dovetail's alternate-answer rule still requires both neighbors known and obeys any revealed prefix; those constraints are stated rather than silently treating every fitting one-neighbor word as a solution.

Older cached clients can still overwrite newer localStorage from a concurrently open tab; this code cannot rewrite an already running old client. No atomic cross-process transaction guarantee is claimed. Physical iPhone touch, accessibility/screen-reader use, hardware timing, long sessions, human enjoyment and final integrated/offline/native packaging remain root or human verification gates. There is no claim of semantic perfection, perfect difficulty, exact WWF parity or universal English completeness.
