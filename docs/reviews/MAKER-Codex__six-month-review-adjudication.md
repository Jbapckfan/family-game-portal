## Concerns and limits retained

Whole-board canonical uniqueness means182 different scheduled challenge configurations per game, not182 independently authored concepts. Counterpart and Second Sense recombine finite analogy/homograph pools; categories, component clues, arches, letter racks and vocabulary recur in other games. Changing the two required Twin Hive letters changes accepted answers, but the rack can recur. These are disclosed content-design limits. Rotation/mirror/recolor-only copies are excluded by mechanic-specific signatures; legacy Switchpath/Prism duplicates remain historically addressable but not in new default practice or the reserved rotation.

Repeated wrong guesses costing a mistake in Quartet/Quadra are existing game-specific rules, not a shared-engine defect. Prism's hypothetical all-constant rule does not occur in the shipped bank. Syntax Siege's current waves all use20seconds; supporting heterogeneous future durations is not claimed. Claude Tangle's event cap is a bounded-save policy. Old malformed saves do not establish a published-format compatibility defect without an actual prior-format example.

All author-run engine, calendar, browser and offline evidence is separate from provider review. Browser automation is source-assisted through actual controls and includes save/reload; it is not a blind human difficulty study. Physical iPhone touch/audio/haptics and a human editorial pass remain unverified. The bonus is generated play with two AI levels, not a daily content bank; exhaustive board checks and162 complete opening/AI-level matches are its evidence, not a claim of perfect AI play.

Fresh daily/practice rounds still receive reviewed vocabulary.
