    A note from Alford Daily

    A small daily ritual.

    Alford Daily brings together 32 word, logic, memory, and strategy games. Ringcount and Switchpath are quick first stops; choose any game or filter the collection by its original creator.

    Original creators, always credited

    ChatGPT developed Ringcount, Switchpath, Counterpart, Shortlist, Second Sense, Sideways, Tradeoff, and Inbetween. Kimi developed Cairn, Morph, Tangle, Hunch, Circuit, and Quartet in the Pebbles package. Integration and repairs by ChatGPT do not change those credits. DeepSeek supplied the concepts for Twin Hive, Spare, Rung, Cipher, Trail, and Split. Qwen supplied Quadra and the concepts for Logic Lattice, Syntax Siege, Memory Maze, Pattern Prism, and Territory Tactics. Games completed from those concepts show separate ChatGPT implementation credit. Claude developed Dovetail, Splice, Sprout, Tangle, Keystone, and Tumbler in Foreword. Both versions of Tangle keep their own creator labels and progress.

    One day, the same puzzles

    Everyone gets the same daily board for each game. The day changes at midnight in America/Chicago (Central Time). Most puzzles work at your own pace. Syntax Siege adds timed word waves, with untimed assistance and practice available.

    Pick up where you left off

    Progress is saved in this browser on this device. This home counts a daily game as solved only when you solve it; revealed answers and practice rounds do not increase the daily count. Separate website addresses have separate saves.

    More room to play

    Every game has at least 120 prepared puzzles. Choose Practice to keep playing whenever you like; practice has its own result and never changes your daily record. These collections are finite and will eventually repeat, and some word games reuse curated words in different combinations. Imported collections may include familiar word-game formats.

    Just the puzzles, for now

    The games contain no advertising, purchase prompts, or tracking code. If browser storage is unavailable, a game can still be played, but progress may not survive closing the page.
