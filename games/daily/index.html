    A note from Alford Daily

    A small daily ritual.

    Alford Daily brings together six word and logic games, with Ringcount and Switchpath as the quick first stop. Tradeoff and Inbetween are here as two family favorites, too.

    One day, the same puzzles

    Everyone gets the same daily board for each game. The day changes at midnight in America/Chicago (Central Time). Play at your own pace; there is no hard timer.

    Pick up where you left off

    Progress is saved in this browser on this device. This home counts a daily game as solved only when you solve it; revealed answers and practice rounds do not increase the daily count. Separate website addresses have separate saves.

    More room to play

    Each game has a prepared collection of boards and a practice mode. These collections are finite and will eventually repeat. Your unfinished daily board stays reserved for daily play.

    Just the puzzles, for now

    This build has no accounts, advertising, purchase prompts, or tracking code. If browser storage is unavailable, a game can still be played, but progress may not survive closing the page.
