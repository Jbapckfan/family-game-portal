House rules

How to play ERS

  1. On your turn, tap your face-down deck to flip one card onto the center pile.
  2. Slap a valid pattern first to capture the whole pile—even if you ran out of cards.
  3. A bad slap burns up to two of your cards into the prize pile.
  4. Face cards start challenges: Jack gives 1 chance, Queen 2, King 3, and Ace 4. Reveal another face card to pass the challenge; otherwise the challenger captures the pile.
  5. Keyboard: Space or Enter flips your card, S slaps, P pauses.
