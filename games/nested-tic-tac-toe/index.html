NINE BOARDS / ONE MATCH

How to play

  1. You are the blue ×. The AI is the teal ○. You make the first move in any mini board.
  2. Choose a board in the overview, then play a square in the larger view. Your chosen small square sends the opponent to the matching big board: top left sends them to top left, center sends them to center.
  3. Three marks in a mini board claim that big square. Three claimed squares in a big-board line win the match.
  4. Won and drawn mini boards are closed. If the next destination is closed, choose any unfinished board. Drawn boards cannot be claimed.
  5. If every board closes without a big-board winning line, the match is a draw.

Pick Casual or Tactical before your first move. Both levels run locally and make deterministic choices. Your match resumes on this device, including an interrupted AI turn.

Keyboard: Tab to choose a board or square. Enter or Space selects. Arrow keys move between available squares.
