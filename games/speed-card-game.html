Rules

How to play Speed

  1. Tap any glowing card that is exactly one rank higher or lower than either center card.
  2. Aces connect both ways: King ↔ Ace ↔ 2. Suits do not matter.
  3. Your hand refills automatically. There are no turns—the bot plays at the same time.
  4. If nobody can move, new center cards flip automatically. First player with zero cards wins.
  5. Keyboard: use 1–5 for your cards, P to pause, and R to restart.
