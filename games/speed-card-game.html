Rules

How to play Speed

  1. Choose a card, then choose the exact center pile where you want to play it. Or drag the card directly onto that pile.
  2. For a faster move, double-click the left or right half of a card to send it straight to the matching center pile.
  3. Aces connect both ways: King ↔ Ace ↔ 2. Suits do not matter.
  4. Your hand refills automatically. There are no turns—the bot plays at the same time.
  5. If nobody can move, new center cards flip automatically. First player with zero cards wins.
  6. In 2 Player, the top player plays from the opposite side or uses Q–T; Player 1 uses 1–5.
  7. Variants can add wild stars, same-rank doubles, a third center pile, or shorter sudden-death decks.
  8. Keyboard: choose a card with 1–5 or Q–T, then choose a pile with A/S/D. P pauses and R restarts.
