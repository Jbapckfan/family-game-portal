How placing works

→SPRING
  1. Grab a pair. It turns blue and every gap widens.
  2. Drop it in a gap (or tap the gap). The blue letters show your preview, in order.
  3. Place pair to confirm. Previews are free; a wrong confirmed fit costs one attempt.

Keyboard: Tab to a pair, Enter; arrow keys move between gaps; Enter previews.
