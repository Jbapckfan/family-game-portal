How a level is marked

  • Clean — solved with no misses or hints.
  • Assisted — solved after a miss or hint.
  • Shown — skipped; not counted as solved.

What happens next

Solve this word and a fourth letter joins the rack. The next stair is one slot wider; any valid word using every letter counts.

Keyboard

Type letters, Enter to submit, ⌫ to remove. Editing and shuffling are free; a full invalid word is a miss.

Concept notes: the climb is the board. Solved stairs keep their word engraved with the status mark on the slab edge; no separate summit illustration. Rack letters T·H·A are today's supplied Level 1 sample. Prototype wiring: tapping tiles and placed letters and the preview toggle work; typing, Enter, Shuffle, Delete, Hint, Skip and the mode switch are visual only.
