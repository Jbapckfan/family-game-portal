Arc 1 · NEKBC

Concept notes: the chain is drawn as numbered chords across the ring — a legal word always zig-zags between quadrants, so the rule becomes visible geometry. Bands stay split exactly on the cardinal axes. Illustrative state: HAWK played; tap letters to extend the dashed word (same-arc taps are refused with a reason).
