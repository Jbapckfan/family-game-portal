Concept notes: for phones, the ring becomes a compact map and input moves to a 2×2 keypad laid out in the same compass positions (Arc 4 NW, Arc 1 NE, Arc 3 SW, Arc 2 SE) with 56px keys. The quadrant you just used greys out and says why. Illustrative state: HAWK played, next word seeded with K. Keypad is live locally. Arc colours are unchanged; gold keys now use ink letters and the quadrant labels use darker shades of each arc colour for legibility on warm white. Prototype wiring: keypad, ⌫ and Clear work; Play word, Undo, Restart, the route clue, Reveal and the mode switch are visual only.
