Pegs are sorted — blacks first, then whites — and never line up with a letter. Guess 3 tells you three letters are exactly placed and one more is in the word somewhere else, but not which. Repeated letters count only as often as they appear in the answer.

Concept notes: a physical deduction board — neutral letter pegs, and a recessed well of five key-peg holes filled black-then-white. No per-letter colour anywhere, which keeps Hunch clearly unlike colour-tile word games. The answer behind this illustrative practice state is not shown; dot counts are computed consistently for it.
