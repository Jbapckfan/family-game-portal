Reading a row

Counts describe the whole guess. They are sorted, so they never say which letter.

ExactElsewhere

Example from the rules — answer APPLE, guess ALLEY: one exact (A); L and E are in the word elsewhere. The second L doesn't count — APPLE has only one.

Repeated or unrecognised guesses are free to edit and don't use a try.

Concept notes: typographic and adult — guesses in a letter-spaced DM Sans ledger with two numbered columns, numerals for precision and dots for scanning. On 320px phones the second column keeps its numeral and drops the dot repeat. Practice state is illustrative; the ALLEY example's counts follow the supplied rules. Prototype wiring: none of the input is wired — the keyboard, typing, Enter, ⌫, Reveal and the mode switch are visual only.
