Illustrative only — rows 4–5 are invented to show a finished card. The card shares grouped dots per guess, never letters, so it can't spoil the word and doesn't resemble a coloured letter grid.

Concept notes: hierarchy flips — the guess you are writing is the largest object, earlier guesses compress into a newest-first list beneath. Large dock keyboard for phones; typing on a physical keyboard works the same.
