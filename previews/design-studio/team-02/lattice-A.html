Concept notes: undecided squares are plain (no "?" icons, so it stops reading as Minesweeper). Every total is a live counter (black so far / needed) and turns green or red. Selecting a total draws a band over its five squares; the ↘ total sits at the top-left corner where its diagonal starts and the ↙ total at the bottom-left where its diagonal ends. Totals and givens are today's supplied puzzle; marking is local.
