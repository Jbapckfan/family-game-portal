Concept notes: pieces become anodised machined pucks with an engraved shape and a rim notch for direction, seated in recessed wells on one rail — adult, physical, and unambiguous (a circle's direction is the notch, not a guess). The decoder table splits the four signals into rows so cycles become readable. Sequence 01–05 and choices A–D are today's supplied state; locking in is a local preview with no right/wrong shown. "Tune cycles" is an optional mechanic experiment, off by default.
