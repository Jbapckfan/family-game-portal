Press through touching keys, diagonals too. Release on a letter, or Check.

Bonus board · optional opens after Gold

A harder 5×5 web for players who reach Gold. Separate score; it never changes your daily result or streak.

Letters stay hidden until unlocked. This replaces today's "5×5 Preview" tab with an earned, optional round.

Concept notes: warm-white keycaps with a short flat press as you trace, joined by a thin green wire. The medal track ends in an optional bonus step (a proposed placement for the existing 5×5 board — a product decision, not a rule change). Tap adjacent keys to try it; nothing is checked. Prototype wiring: tap-to-trace, Clear and the bonus disclosure work; dragging/release, Check, Clue and the mode switch are visual only.
