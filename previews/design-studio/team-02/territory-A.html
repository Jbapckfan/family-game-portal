Concept notes: the exact target is the headline, remaining stones are physical tokens, and an inspect tap shows a group's size and openings directly on the board. The before/after lesson stays in context and collapses once learned. Board is today's supplied 37-hex position; placements and captures run as a local rule preview (including the "own group needs an opening" rule) and are not the game's validation. Zoom enlarges hexes to 52px and pans inside the board frame, never the page. Blue stones and amber targets keep their game colours (flattened, no gloss); openings are marked in home green on the board and in the lesson. Prototype wiring: inspect, placing, the local capture preview, Undo, Restart, Zoom and the lesson disclosure work; hint, reveal and the mode switch are visual only.
