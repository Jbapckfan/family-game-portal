Concept notes: a compact 3·2·3 comb with fixed cell size (76px, 68px under 360px) — it never stretches with the viewport. The two gold cells touch at the centre, and the entry bar has two sockets that fill as A and M are typed. Tap cells to try it. Found words are illustrative, not checked. Prototype wiring: tapping cells, Delete, Clear and the live rule line work; physical typing, Add word, hint, reveal and the mode switch are visual only.
