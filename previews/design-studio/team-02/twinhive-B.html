Concept notes: the six-word goal is a numbered ledger — slots, not loose chips — so progress and points read like a scorecard. The rack is a fixed 4×2 grid of 64px keys (gold first); it cannot stretch. Live checks update as you type. Collection entries are illustrative. Prototype wiring: rack keys, the text field and the live checks work; Delete, Clear, Add word, hint, reveal and the mode switch are visual only.
