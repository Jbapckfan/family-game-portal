Concept notes: each accepted word becomes a physical cell in a six-cell comb, so the collection and the goal are one object. Cells keep true hexagon proportions at a capped size. "Preview fill animation" drops the current entry into the next cell locally — it is not a dictionary check and nothing is saved. Prototype wiring: letter keys, Delete, Clear and the fill preview work; physical typing, Add word, hint, reveal and the mode switch are visual only.
