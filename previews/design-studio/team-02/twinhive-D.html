Concept notes: the classic seven-cell flower, but the centre is one gold hexagon split into A and M halves (each half ≥40×92px) — the "twin" requirement is a single object. Cell size is fixed (88px; 80px under 360px). Letter ticks under the entry preview points: ink for the first three letters, gold for each scoring letter. Collection is illustrative. Prototype wiring: petals, both core halves, Delete, Clear and the ticks work; physical typing, Add word, hint, reveal and the mode switch are visual only.
